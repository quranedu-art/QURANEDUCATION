Common questions

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Fill in the short form and send it to us on WhatsApp. We reply, match you with a tutor and schedule your first class. The trial is free for 7 days. If you decide not to continue, simply tell us.

Can I cancel or change my plan?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time.

What do I need for the classes?

A phone, tablet or computer with internet. Classes run on Zoom or Skype, which are free. A headset helps with sound quality.

Are female tutors available?

Yes. Female students and children can choose a female tutor when they book.

What ages do you teach?

From children aged 4 to adults of any age.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Most students begin with the Arabic alphabet and move forward at their own pace.

What if I don't like my tutor?

Tell us and we will arrange a different tutor.

How do I pay after the trial?

We will confirm payment options with you on WhatsApp before your trial ends. Please see our Refund Policy for details.
