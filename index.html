FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 7-day free trial work?
You can take full classes for 7 days completely free with no credit card required. After 7 days, you choose a weekly plan that suits you. If you decide not to continue, simply do nothing — you will never be charged.
Can I change or cancel my plan at any time?
Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time with no penalties. We believe in complete flexibility for our students and families.
What technology do I need for the classes?
You need a computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access. Classes are conducted via Zoom or Skype, both of which are free to download. A headset is recommended for better audio quality.
Are there female tutors available for female students?
Yes. We have qualified, certified female tutors available for all female students and children. You can request a male or female tutor when you register.
What age groups do you teach?
We teach students of all ages, from children as young as 4 years old to adults of any age. Our tutors are experienced with both young learners and adult students.
What if I am not satisfied with my tutor?
No problem at all. You can request a new tutor at any time, as many times as needed, until you find the perfect match. Student satisfaction is our top priority.
Do you offer classes for complete beginners?
Yes. Most of our students start as complete beginners. Our tutors are experienced in teaching from absolute zero — starting with the Arabic alphabet and progressing at your own pace.
How do I pay? What currencies are accepted?
We accept all major credit and debit cards as well as PayPal. Payments are processed in USD. After your free trial, you pay weekly — there are no monthly commitments or annual fees.
